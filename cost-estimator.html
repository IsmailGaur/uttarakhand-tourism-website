💡 Money Saving Tips

  • Book accommodations in advance for better rates
  • Travel during off-season for discounted prices
  • Use public transportation for local travel
  • Try local street food for authentic and affordable meals
  • Look for package deals that include multiple activities

📝 Important Notes

  • Costs are estimated and may vary based on season and availability
  • Peak season (May-June, Oct-Nov) prices may be 20-30% higher
  • Adventure activities and helicopter services cost extra
  • Always keep 15-20% buffer for unexpected expenses
  • Prices include basic sightseeing but not premium experiences
