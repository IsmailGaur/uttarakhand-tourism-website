Kedarnath

Sacred Jyotirlinga Shrine at 3,583 meters

About Kedarnath

Kedarnath is one of the most sacred Hindu pilgrimage sites, housing one of the twelve Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva. Located at an altitude of 3,583 meters in the Rudraprayag district, this ancient temple is surrounded by breathtaking snow-capped peaks and pristine glacial valleys.

The temple, believed to be over 1,000 years old, is part of the revered Char Dham Yatra and Panch Kedar circuit. The journey to Kedarnath is both spiritually rewarding and physically challenging, offering devotees a chance to experience divine tranquility amidst the majestic Himalayas.

📍 How to Reach

By Air

Nearest airport: Jolly Grant Airport, Dehradun (239 km)

By Train

Nearest railway station: Rishikesh (221 km) or Haridwar (245 km)

By Road

Drive to Gaurikund (16 km trek to temple) or take helicopter from Phata/Sersi

🛌 Where to Stay

Kedarnath

Basic guesthouses and dharamshalas near the temple (₹500-1500/night)

Gaurikund

Hotels and lodges at the trek starting point (₹800-2500/night)

Guptkashi

Comfortable hotels 30 km before Gaurikund (₹1200-4000/night)

🎯 What to Do

  • 🛕 Visit the ancient Kedarnath Temple and attend evening aarti
  • 🥾 Trek through scenic mountain trails and glacial valleys
  • 📷 Photography of stunning Himalayan peaks and landscapes
  • 🧘 Meditation and spiritual practices in serene environment
  • 🏔️ Explore nearby Vasuki Tal and Gandhi Sarovar lakes
  • 🌅 Witness breathtaking sunrise over snow-capped mountains

⛺ Ideal For

🛕 Spiritual Journey 🥾 Adventure Trekking 📷 Photography 🏔️ Mountain Experience

📅 Best Time to Visit

May to October: Temple is open for darshan. Weather is pleasant but can be unpredictable. Carry warm clothes even in summer.

November to April: Temple remains closed due to heavy snowfall. The deity is shifted to Ukhimath during winter months.

🗺️ Nearby Places

Gaurikund (16 km)

Hot springs and starting point of Kedarnath trek

Vasuki Tal (8 km)

High-altitude lake with stunning mountain reflections

Chorabari Tal (3 km)

Gandhi Sarovar - peaceful glacial lake

Quick Facts

Altitude: 3,583 meters
Trek Distance: 16 km from Gaurikund
Temple Timing: 4:00 AM - 9:00 PM
Best Season: May - October
Difficulty: Moderate to Difficult

Weather

Summer (May-Jun): 5°C to 18°C
Monsoon (Jul-Sep): 8°C to 15°C
Winter (Oct-Apr): -15°C to 5°C

Important Tips

  • Carry warm clothes and rain gear
  • Book helicopter tickets in advance
  • Start trek early morning
  • Carry oxygen cylinders if needed
  • Respect temple customs and traditions
