Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best time to visit Uttarakhand?

The best time depends on your destination and activities. For hill stations and trekking: March-June and September-November. For Char Dham: May-October. For skiing: December-March.

Do I need permits for trekking?

Some high-altitude treks and protected areas require permits. We can help you obtain necessary permits for Valley of Flowers, Nanda Devi, and other restricted areas.

How do I book helicopter services for Char Dham?

Helicopter bookings can be made online through official websites or authorized agents. We recommend booking well in advance, especially during peak season.

What should I pack for a Uttarakhand trip?

Pack layers of clothing, comfortable walking shoes, rain gear, sunscreen, first aid kit, and any personal medications. Specific gear depends on your planned activities.
